Search "best CRM for accountants Australia" and you will mostly find software directories. Long lists. Star ratings. Very little help deciding.

The real question is simpler. Which problem are you trying to fix?

## Two different jobs, often confused

Accounting firms need software for two separate jobs.

**Running client work.** Jobs, deadlines, workpapers, lodgements, billing. This is practice management. Tools such as TaxDome and the major practice platforms are built for it.

**Winning new clients.** Answering enquiries, booking first meetings, following up proposals, collecting reviews. This is client acquisition. It is where most firms quietly lose revenue.

Many firms buy a practice management system, call it their CRM, and wonder why new client numbers do not move. The system was never designed to answer a missed call at 6 pm in July.

## Where firms actually lose new clients

The pattern is the same across most Australian practices:

- **Calls missed in tax time.** Staff are with clients. The phone rings out.
- **Slow replies to web enquiries.** A form sits unread for two days. The prospect books elsewhere.
- **Proposals not chased.** An engagement letter goes out. Nobody follows up.
- **No review strategy.** Happy clients are never asked, so the firm sits low in local search.
- **Referrals not tracked.** Nobody knows which partners send the best clients.

None of these are accounting problems. They are follow-up problems.

## What to look for in an accountant CRM

Use this checklist when you compare options.

1. **Answers every enquiry fast.** Look for an AI agent that replies to calls, chats and forms in minutes, day or night.
2. **Books meetings without phone tag.** Online booking that writes straight into your calendar.
3. **Chases proposals for you.** Automated email and SMS until the client signs.
4. **Asks for Google reviews.** Automatically, after each engagement.
5. **Shows where clients come from.** Referral partners, Google, social and ads, side by side.
6. **Sits beside your practice software.** It should not replace it, and it should connect where an API is available.
7. **Published Australian pricing.** No quote-only sales process for a small firm.
8. **A local team accountable for it.** Someone you can call who knows Australian tax seasons.
