Tax time breaks the front desk of most accounting firms.

Calls double. Staff are with clients. Callers hear voicemail, hang up and ring the next firm on Google. Most will never leave a message.

An AI receptionist fixes that. But the market is new, and most of the tools ranking for "AI receptionist for accountants" in Australia are built and supported overseas.

Here is how to choose well.

## What an AI receptionist should do for a firm

At minimum, it should:

- **Answer every call**, day, night and weekends, in a natural voice.
- **Answer common questions** such as fees, deadlines, documents to bring and office hours.
- **Book appointments** straight into your calendar and send confirmations.
- **Hand over complex or urgent calls** to your team with a summary.
- **Log every call** against the client record, so nothing is lost.

If a tool only takes messages, it is a voicemail with better manners.

## Seven questions to ask before you buy

1. **Who supports it, and in which time zone?** When something goes wrong in the last week of October, you need help in Australian business hours.
2. **Does it know Australian context?** Tax time, BAS and lodgement deadlines, and local phone numbers.
3. **Where do call records go?** Summaries should land in your CRM, not a separate inbox.
4. **Can it book, or only take messages?** Booking is where the value is.
5. **How does handover work?** Urgent calls need a clean transfer, not a dead end.
6. **How is usage priced?** Look for included minutes and a monthly budget you control.
7. **Does it connect to follow-up?** A booked call should trigger reminders and next steps automatically.
