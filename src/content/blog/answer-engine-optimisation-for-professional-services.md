Answer engine optimisation (AEO) is the work of making your firm's website clear enough that AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can understand it, trust it and name you in their answers. For a professional services firm, that means plain answers to client questions, clear services and prices, and consistent details everywhere.

It is not a separate trick. It is good SEO, written so a machine can quote it.

## Why does answer engine optimisation matter for professional services firms?

Because more of your future clients now ask an AI assistant before they search or call. If the assistant cannot work out what your firm does, where you are and who you help, it names another firm. "Who is a good accountant near me?" is now answered in a paragraph.

The [Telsyte Australian Artificial Intelligence Study 2026](https://www.telsyte.com.au/announcements/2026/6/2/four-in-five-ai-users-now-engage-monthly-as-australias-user-base-surges-past-17-million), published on 2 June 2026, found:

- 17.4 million Australians, or 77% of people aged 16 and over, now use AI.
- 12% of Australians say AI tools are now their main way of finding information online, up from 5% a year earlier.
- 81% are aware of AI summaries in search results, and half say they would often rely on the summary without clicking through.

Google is still the main entry point, but more of the decision now happens inside the answer.

## Is answer engine optimisation different from SEO?

They overlap heavily. SEO helps your pages rank in search results. Answer engine optimisation makes the content on those pages easy to lift and quote inside an AI answer. The foundations are the same: pages that can be crawled and indexed, helpful content, and accurate business details. The difference is mostly in how you write and structure each page.

Google says this directly. Its [guide to AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) (last updated 10 December 2025) states that the best practices for SEO remain relevant, that a page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet to appear as a supporting link, and that there is no special schema or AI text file required to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode.

Here is how the two compare in practice:

| | Traditional SEO | Answer engine optimisation |
|---|---|---|
| Main goal | Rank a page in the results list | Be named or quoted inside the answer |
| Starting point | Keywords and search volume | The questions clients actually ask |
| Page structure | Long pages built around a keyword | A direct answer first, then the detail |
| What wins | Relevance, links and page quality | Clear, consistent, quotable facts |
| Prices and process | Often left vague | Stated plainly, because buyers ask |
| How you measure | Rankings, clicks and traffic | Mentions in AI answers, enquiries and booked meetings |

## What do AI assistants look for when they recommend a firm?

They look for clear, consistent and trustworthy facts. That means a plain statement of what your firm does, who it serves and where, answers to common client questions, visible prices or price ranges, and the same name, address and phone number on your website and listings. Vague, image-heavy or contradictory pages tend to be skipped.

For professional services, the gaps we see most often are simple:

- The home page says "we help you achieve your goals" but never says "tax returns for sole traders in Parramatta".
- Fees are "on application", so the assistant cannot answer a price question and names a firm that can.
- The firm's opening hours, phone number or suburb differ between the website, Google Business Profile and directories.
- Services are listed in one paragraph instead of a page per service.

None of these need a new website, just clearer writing.

## What does a simple answer engine optimisation checklist look like?

Start with ten client questions and answer each one plainly on your website. Put the answer in the first sentence under a question heading, add the detail underneath, keep your business details identical everywhere, and check your pages are indexed. Then ask the same questions in the AI assistants each month and record who is named.

A practical first month for an accounting, bookkeeping, advice or broking firm:

1. **List the questions.** Pull ten real questions from your inbox and calls, such as "how much does a tax return cost for a sole trader".
2. **Answer first.** Give each question a heading and a 40 to 60 word direct answer, then the detail.
3. **One page per service.** Each saying who it is for and where you work.
4. **Show prices or ranges.** Even "from" pricing helps an assistant answer a cost question.
5. **Match your details.** Same firm name, address, phone and hours on your site, Google Business Profile and every directory.
6. **Add structured data that matches the page.** FAQ and organisation markup should repeat what is visible, never more.
7. **Check indexing.** Use Google Search Console to confirm each new page is on Google.
8. **Collect reviews.** Ask happy clients for Google reviews. Our guide to [getting more Google reviews](/blog/more-google-reviews) shows a simple routine.
9. **Reply fast.** Being named is only half the job. Our guide to [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead) explains why the first reply decides who wins the client.
10. **Measure monthly.** Ask the same questions in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity, and record who is named.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does AEO stand for?

AEO stands for answer engine optimisation. It means making your website clear enough that AI assistants and AI search summaries can understand your firm and include it in their answers.

### Do I need special files or code for AI Overviews?

Not for Google. Google's guidance says there are no extra technical requirements, special schema or AI text files needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Your pages need to be indexed, helpful and accurate, with structured data that matches the visible text.

### Can anyone guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my firm?

No. AI answers change, and no one controls them. What you can do is improve what assistants read about your firm and measure the answers every month, so you can see whether it is working.

### How long does answer engine optimisation take?

New pages are usually indexed within a few weeks. AI answers change more slowly, so track the same questions monthly, alongside enquiries and booked meetings.
