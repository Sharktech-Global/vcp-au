To market a new business in Australia from scratch, build in this order: get found (Google Business Profile, a fast website, search and AI answers), earn trust (reviews and clear proof), then capture and follow up every enquiry automatically. Most new businesses do the first part and skip the follow-up, which is where first clients are lost.

## Why is it hard for a new business to win its first clients?

A new business starts with no reviews, no search history and no referrals. Buyers compare several options and choose the one that looks established and replies first. Competition is also heavy: hundreds of thousands of Australian businesses start every year, so being good at the work is not enough to be noticed.

The Australian Bureau of Statistics counted 460,461 new businesses in 2025-26, with 375,331 exits, and 2,814,778 actively trading businesses at 30 June 2026.

The founders who break through are rarely the loudest. They are the easiest to find, trust and contact.

## What should a new business set up first?

Set up the basics that let a buyer find you, check you and contact you in one sitting. That means a Google Business Profile, a fast website with clear pricing or next steps, a booking calendar, a way to capture every call and message, and a system that asks happy clients for reviews. Advertising comes after these work.

Here is the order we recommend for founders starting from zero:

1. **Google Business Profile.** Free, and often the first thing a local buyer sees.
2. **A fast, clear website.** One page per service, a plain explanation of who you help, and one obvious next step.
3. **Enquiry capture.** Calls, forms, chat, Facebook and Instagram messages all land in one inbox and one CRM.
4. **Instant replies and booking.** An AI agent answers, qualifies and books the call, even at night.
5. **Reviews.** An automatic request after every job.
6. **Content that answers questions.** Articles and pages that Google and AI tools can quote.
7. **Social media.** Consistent posts that point back to your booking page.
8. **Paid ads.** Only once the steps above work.

The [marketing guidance on business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/marketing-and-advertising) starts with knowing your target market and writing a plan. Do that first, then build the system that acts on it.

## Visibility, credibility, prosperity: what does each stage need?

Think of marketing a new business as three stages. Visibility gets you found. Credibility makes the buyer choose you. Prosperity comes from capturing, following up and keeping every client you have earned. Each stage needs different tools, and skipping one breaks the chain.

| Stage | The question a buyer asks | What you need | What it looks like when it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visibility | Can I find you? | Website, SEO, answer engine optimisation, Google Business Profile, social media | You appear when someone searches or asks an AI tool for your service |
| Credibility | Can I trust you? | Reviews, clear pricing, a real team, consistent profiles | Buyers contact you without needing a referral |
| Prosperity | Will you look after me? | CRM, instant replies, booking, follow-up, AI agents, reporting | No enquiry is missed and you know which channel brings clients |

## How does a new business show up in Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini?

Search engines and AI assistants both reward pages that answer real questions clearly. Write pages that open with a direct answer, cover one topic each and use question headings. Keep your business details the same everywhere, collect genuine reviews, and get listed on credible directories. AI tools quote sources they can read and trust.

A few practical rules:

- **Answer first.** Put a short, plain answer at the top of every page and under every heading.
- **Use real text, not images.** Prices, services and proof trapped in an image cannot be read by search engines or AI tools.
- **Be consistent.** The same business name, address, phone number and description on your website, Google profile and directories.
- **Earn mentions.** Reviews and directory listings build the trust AI answers rely on.

## How fast should a new business reply to an enquiry?

Reply within minutes. A buyer who has just found a new business is usually comparing two or three options, and the first clear reply often wins the work. Founders are pitching, delivering and doing the admin, so manual replies slip. An AI agent can answer, qualify and book the call while you work.

## Should you do it yourself, hire, or use a managed growth system?

Most founders choose between doing it themselves with separate tools, hiring freelancers or an agency for parts of the job, hiring a marketer, or using one managed system. The right choice depends on your time, budget and how many tools you are willing to run.

| Approach | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Do it yourself with separate tools | Very early testing on a small budget | Many logins and bills, and leads lost between tools |
| Freelancers or an agency for parts of the job | One specific project, such as a logo or a website | Nobody owns the whole journey from first click to signed client |
| Hiring an in-house marketer | Businesses with steady revenue | Salary cost before you have the pipeline to justify it |
| A managed growth system | Founders who want it built and run for them | Choose one with published pricing and an Australian team you can call |

## What are the rules for email and SMS marketing in Australia?

Australian spam rules apply from your first campaign. You need consent before sending marketing emails or SMS, you must identify your business with correct contact details, and every message needs a working unsubscribe option that you honour within five working days. Build these into your CRM from day one.

The rules are set out on the Australian Communications and Media Authority [avoid sending spam](https://www.acma.gov.au/node/73) page. A good CRM records consent and handles unsubscribes automatically.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest way to market a new business in Australia?

Start with a free Google Business Profile, a simple website and a habit of asking every client for a review. Then add a low-cost system that captures and replies to every enquiry, so the visibility you earn is not wasted.

### How long does it take for a new business to show up on Google?

It varies. A Google Business Profile can appear quickly once verified, while website pages often take weeks to months to rank. Publishing answer-first pages regularly and earning reviews speeds up the process.

### Do I need social media if I have a website?

Yes, for most businesses. Social media shows the work and the people behind it. It works best when every post points to a page where buyers can book.

### What is answer engine optimisation?

Answer engine optimisation, or AEO, is structuring your content so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can quote it. It relies on clear answers, question headings and trusted mentions of your business.
