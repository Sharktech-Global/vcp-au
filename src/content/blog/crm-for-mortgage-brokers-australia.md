Australian mortgage brokers are well served for loan software.

Platforms such as Salestrekker and BrokerOS, and the tools provided by aggregators, handle origination, applications and lender submissions. They are built for compliance and process.

But most brokers lose deals before any of that starts.

## The deal is decided in the first reply

A borrower ready to buy rarely contacts one broker. They fill in two or three forms, message a couple of Facebook pages and call whoever answers.

The first broker to reply usually gets the conversation. The rest get silence.

Loan software does not help here. It assumes the client is already yours.

## Where brokers lose leads

- **After-hours enquiries.** Borrowers research at night. Replies the next morning are late.
- **Leads in too many places.** Website, Facebook, Instagram, referral partners and portals all land in different inboxes.
- **No follow-up for "not yet" borrowers.** Someone six months from buying is forgotten by month two.
- **Past clients left alone.** Refinance and repeat opportunities slip because nobody reaches out.

## Two layers, one pipeline

Think of your systems in two layers.

| Layer | Job | Typical tools |
|---|---|---|
| Lead capture and conversion | Reply first, qualify, book, nurture, ask for reviews | VCPility |
| Loan management | Fact find, origination, lodgement, compliance | Your broker CRM and aggregator platform |

The first layer feeds the second. Keep both, and connect them where an API is available.

## What to look for in the lead layer

1. **Replies in minutes, around the clock.** An AI agent that answers web, social and phone enquiries.
2. **Asks the qualifying questions.** Purchase or refinance, timeframe, rough borrowing need.
3. **Books a discovery call** straight into your calendar.
4. **One inbox for every lead source.**
5. **Long-term nurture** for borrowers who are not ready yet.
6. **Timed outreach to past clients** when it is worth reviewing their loan.
7. **Google review requests** after settlement.
