Australian advice practices already run serious software.

Advice platforms such as AdviserLogic, the large global CRMs with financial services editions, and newer tools such as Plannr handle client records, fact finds, compliance and reviews.

What they rarely do well is the part before someone becomes a client.

## The gap between enquiry and first meeting

People take a long time to choose a financial planner. They research, compare, read reviews and wait.

During that time, a practice needs to:

- Reply quickly when someone first enquires.
- Book a first appointment while interest is high.
- Stay in touch, helpfully, until the prospect is ready.
- Know which seminars, referrals and ads actually produce clients.

This is client acquisition. It is a different job from advice software.

## Where practices lose prospects

- **Enquiries arrive in the evening.** Replies the next day feel slow.
- **Long decision cycles.** Without follow-up, prospects drift to whoever stayed in touch.
- **Seminar leads go cold.** Registrations are collected, then nothing happens.
- **Marketing is not measured.** Spend continues on channels that never produce a client.

## What to look for

1. **Fast first response** to web and social enquiries, with booking built in.
2. **Nurture sequences you approve.** Nothing should send without sign-off.
3. **Event and seminar follow-up** that runs automatically.
4. **A prospect pipeline** from first enquiry to signed client.
5. **Review and referral prompts** at the right moments.
6. **Source reporting** that ties marketing spend to appointments.
7. **Works alongside your advice software**, not instead of it.
